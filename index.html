What We Do

Our Services

We handle every phase of your masonry and concrete project — from planning to the finished surface.

Concrete Flatwork

Driveways, walkways, patios, and slabs poured and finished to perfection. Built for the desert heat.

Decorative Overlays

Resurface existing concrete with stamped patterns, stained finishes, and texture coatings that refresh any surface.

Paver Installation

Interlocking concrete and natural stone pavers for driveways, pool decks, and patios that stand up to desert conditions.

Retaining Walls

Structural block and stone retaining walls that control erosion, level terrain, and add curb appeal to your property.

Stamped Concrete

Achieve the look of slate, flagstone, or brick at a fraction of the cost with expertly stamped and colored concrete.

Repair & Restoration

Crack filling, resurfacing, and structural repairs to extend the life of existing concrete and masonry work.

About Us

Desert-Tough Craftsmanship

Cambron Masonry is a locally owned and operated business proudly serving Desert Hot Springs and the greater Coachella Valley. We specialize in concrete and masonry work built to handle the desert climate — the heat, the UV, and the shifting soils.

Every project we take on gets the same attention to detail, whether it's a small walkway repair or a full driveway and patio installation. We show up on time, communicate clearly, and don't cut corners.
