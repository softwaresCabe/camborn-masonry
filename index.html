What We Do

Our Services

We handle every phase of your masonry and concrete project — from planning to the finished surface.

Concrete Flatwork

Driveways, walkways, patios, and slabs poured and finished to perfection. Built for the desert heat.

Decorative Overlays

Resurface existing concrete with stamped patterns, stained finishes, and texture coatings that refresh any surface.

Paver Installation

Stamped Concrete

Repair & Restoration

About Us

Desert-Tough Craftsmanship

Camborn Masonry is a locally owned and operated contractor proudly serving Desert Hot Springs and the greater Coachella Valley. We specialize in concrete and masonry work built to handle the desert climate — the heat, the UV, and the shifting soils.

Every project we take on gets the same attention to detail, whether it's a small walkway repair or a full driveway and patio installation. We show up on time, communicate clearly, and don't cut corners.
